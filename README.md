## 🎯 Objective

To analyze business data using a Bubble Chart and other visualizations in Tableau to identify important business patterns, category performance, and trends.

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- Tableau Public
- Business/Superstore Dataset
- Data Visualization
- Interactive Dashboard

---

## 📈 Visualizations

- Bubble Chart for business category analysis
- Category-wise performance chart
- Region-wise analysis
- Sales trend analysis
- Interactive filters

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

1. The Bubble Chart makes it easy to compare the performance of different business categories.
2. Some categories contribute more significantly to overall business performance.
3. Regional performance varies across different areas.
4. Sales performance changes across different categories and time periods.
5. Interactive filters help users analyze specific categories or regions.

---

## 💡 Recommendations

1. Focus on high-performing categories and regions to improve overall business performance.
2. Analyze low-performing categories and identify strategies to increase their sales and profitability.
