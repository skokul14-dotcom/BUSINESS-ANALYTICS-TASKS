# E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard** created using Tableau Public. The dashboard analyzes sales performance based on **time, category, region, and product performance**.

The main objective is to identify important sales trends, understand category and regional performance, and provide useful business recommendations.

---

## 📂 Dataset

The dataset contains e-commerce sales information with the following fields:

- Order Date
- Product Name
- Category
- Region
- Quantity
- Sales
- Profit

The dataset contains **3,500 records** covering the period from **2022 to 2024**.

---

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

The dashboard contains the following visualizations:

### 1. Sales Trend Over Time

A line chart showing how sales vary across different months.

### 2. Sales by Category

A bar chart comparing sales performance across different product categories.

### 3. Sales by Region

A pie chart showing the contribution of each region to total sales.

### 4. Product Performance

A bubble chart used to compare product performance based on sales and profit.

---

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes the following interactive filters:

- **Category**
- **Region**

These filters allow users to explore the dashboard based on specific categories and regions.

---

## 🔍 Key Findings

1. **Electronics is the highest-performing category**, followed by Accessories, while Office has the lowest sales.

2. **West is the top-performing region**, while North has the lowest sales among the four regions.

3. **Sales vary across the months**, showing noticeable differences in monthly sales performance.

---

## 💡 Suggestions / Recommendations

1. The business should focus more marketing and promotional activities on the **Electronics category** while improving the performance of the Office category.

2. The business should focus on improving sales in the **North region** through targeted promotions, customer-focused offers, and region-specific marketing strategies.

---

## ✅ Conclusion

The analysis shows that the e-commerce business performs strongly in the **Electronics category and West region**. However, the Office category and North region provide opportunities for improvement.

By strengthening high-performing areas and addressing weaker areas, the business can improve overall sales performance and make better data-driven decisions.
