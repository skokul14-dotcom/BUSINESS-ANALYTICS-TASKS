## 🎯 Objective

The objective of this task is to analyze business trends over time using Tableau Public and identify important sales patterns.

## 📌 Visualizations Created

1. **Sales Trend Over Time – Line Graph**

   * Shows how sales change over time.
   * Helps identify increasing and decreasing trends, peaks, and drops.

2. **Sales by Category – Bar Chart**

   * Compares sales across different product categories.

3. **Sales by Region – Pie Chart**

   * Shows the distribution of sales across different regions.

4. **Sales by Segment Over Time – Area Chart**

   * Shows sales trends for different customer segments over time.

## 🔍 Interactive Filter

* **Region Filter**
* Allows users to select a specific region and interactively analyze the dashboard.

## 💡 Business Insights

1. Sales change over time with different high and low periods.
2. The line graph helps identify sales peaks and drops.
3. Sales performance varies across different product categories.
4. Sales contribution differs across regions.
5. Different customer segments show different sales patterns over time.

## 📈 Business Recommendations

1. Focus on high-performing categories and regions to maintain strong sales performance.
2. Analyze low-performing periods and regions to identify opportunities for improving sales.
